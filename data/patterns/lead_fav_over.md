# Lead: strong favourite -1.5 + over

Run 2026-11-17. The lead (2025-26 + live): with favourites priced 60%+ to win, 'favourite by 2+ AND over' hit 2.7-4 points more than the product of the two fair prices. It was spotted after looking, so it needs data it did not come from.

Favourite strength for unpriced seasons = a proxy for Pinnacle's moneyline fitted on 2025-26 from point-in-time stats (correlation with the real price r = 0.82). Totals count the shootout goal as books do.

## 4. Verdict

- Fixed line (5.5) in 2022-25: "favourite by 2+" and "over" go together (lift 1.09-1.15, chi-square p < 0.05 in every strength band). At 6.5 they are independent.
- But that link is mostly a lurking variable: high-scoring matchups make BOTH more likely. Books move the total line with expected scoring, which removes it. Against the real lines and Pinnacle's fair prices (2025-26), the pair hit 0.97x / 0.88x / 1.10x the independent product at 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.5 lines: no consistent excess.
- The strong-favourite split is not supported by the independent seasons (no rise in lift with favourite strength), so it was most likely noise.
- Kalshi live, buying favourite -1.5 and the over as two markets: ROI -9% (4.5 line), -5% (5.5), -13% (6.5) over 118 games.
- Empty-net goals are the mechanism people cite and they are real (0.33-0.40 per game; about half of favourite 2+ goal wins include one), but the market already prices the result.
- Conclusion: drop the lead. Not registered as a hypothesis.
